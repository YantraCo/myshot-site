MyShot

Capture a region, annotate it, and get a shareable link on your clipboard — in one keystroke. The file goes to your Google Drive, not ours.

How it works

Three steps
01 Capture Press a shortcut and select a region. MyShot draws its own selector — magnifier, crosshairs, live dimensions, pixel-accurate keyboard nudging — on every platform.
02 Annotate Arrows, boxes, text, highlights, counters and blur, with full undo. Blur properly obscures what is under it, so redactions survive being shared.
03 Share The capture uploads to your Drive, organised by type and month, and the link lands on your clipboard. Offline or signed out, it queues and uploads later — nothing is lost.

Specification

PlatformsLinux (X11 and Wayland), Windows. macOS planned for a later release.
CaptureRegion, full screen, active window. Screen recording to MP4 with hardware encoding.
StorageYour own Google Drive, under your storage quota, owned by you.
Google permissiondrive.file — access limited to files MyShot created. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.
SharingLink-only, never web-indexed. You choose public or restricted before the first upload.
OfflineCaptures queue and upload when a connection returns. Nothing is discarded.
TelemetryNone.
PriceFree.

On privacy

MyShot has no accounts and no servers. Your captures live on your computer and, if you connect Drive, in your own Google account. We never receive them.

Sharing a file publicly means anyone holding the link can view it. Screenshots routinely capture passwords, tokens and customer data, so MyShot asks you to choose before the first upload rather than deciding for you. Full detail in the privacy policy.
