Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

The short version. MyShot is a desktop application. It has no user accounts and no servers. Your screenshots and recordings are stored on your own computer and, if you choose, uploaded to your own Google Drive. We never receive, store or see them. There is no analytics or telemetry of any kind.

1. Who this covers

This policy describes how the MyShot desktop application handles your information. MyShot is developed and maintained by Vikram Yadav, contactable at aryavikram06@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. MyShot operates no servers and transmits no data to the developer. There are no accounts to create, no usage statistics, no crash reporting and no advertising identifiers.

3. Data stored on your computer

MyShot stores the following locally. It never leaves your machine except as described in section 4.

Screenshots and recordingsIn your chosen save folder
ThumbnailsYour OS cache directory
Capture historyA local SQLite database: file paths, sizes, dimensions, timestamps and any Drive link
SettingsA local configuration file
Google sign-in tokenA local file with owner-only permissions, if you connect Google Drive
Diagnostic logsLocal rotating log files, kept on your machine only

4. Google Drive access

Connecting Google Drive is optional. MyShot works fully without it.

What we request

drive.fileAccess limited to files MyShot itself creates. MyShot cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Drive.
openid, emailYour email address, shown in the application so you can tell which account is connected

How uploads work

Files are uploaded directly from your computer to your Google Drive account. They are stored in your Drive, count against your Google storage, and remain owned by you. They do not pass through any MyShot server, because none exists.

Sharing links

MyShot can mark an uploaded file as viewable by anyone holding its link. Before the first upload you are asked to choose, and you can change it at any time in Settings.

Please read this carefully. A public link means anyone who has the URL can view that file. The file is not listed or searchable, but it is not private either. Screenshots frequently capture passwords, access tokens, personal data and confidential information. If you are unsure, choose the restricted option.

5. Third parties

The only third party involved is Google, and only if you connect Drive. Your use of Google Drive is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. MyShot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we do not have it.

6. Children

MyShot is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google: sign out in MyShot, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove local data: delete MyShot's configuration, data and cache folders. Uninstalling removes the application; your captures remain until you delete them.
  • Remove uploaded files: delete them from your Google Drive as you would any other file. Deleting a file also disables its shared link.

There is no account to close and nothing for us to erase on your behalf.

8. Security

Your Google sign-in token is stored with owner-only file permissions. MyShot requests the narrowest Drive permission available so that a compromise of your token cannot expose files MyShot did not create. That said, data on your computer is ultimately as secure as your computer and user account.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will be updated and the change noted in the application's release notes. If MyShot ever introduces server-side accounts, this policy will be revised and published before that release ships.
