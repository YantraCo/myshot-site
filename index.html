यन्त्र

Instruments, not platforms.

Yantra is Sanskrit for instrument — a device built for one job, done exactly. That is the whole brief. We make small, precise software and finish it.

What we make

Three products
01 MyShot Screenshots and screen recording that upload to your own Google Drive and put a shareable link on your clipboard. One keystroke, no account, no servers of ours. Desktop · Linux, Windows In development
02 Product Search for Shopify Storefront search that reads intent rather than matching keywords, so shoppers find the thing they were describing instead of the thing they spelled correctly. Shopify app In development
03 Conversion Tracking for Shopify Marketing attribution and analytics for merchants who need to know which spend actually produced the order, not which channel claimed it last. Shopify app In development

How we build

Four commitments

Your data stays yours

MyShot uploads to the Drive account you already own. Files count against your storage, stay under your control, and never touch a server of ours — because we do not run one.

The narrowest permission

We ask for the least access that does the job. MyShot requests Google's drive.file scope, which can only ever see files the app itself created — never the rest of your Drive.

No telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics, no advertising identifiers. Nothing phones home. We find out how the software behaves by using it ourselves.

Small and finished

Three tools that work beat one platform that almost does. Each product is scoped to a job a person can describe in a sentence, then built until it is genuinely done.
