MyShot

Cross-platform screenshot and screen recording with native Google Drive sharing. Capture, annotate, and get a shareable link on your clipboard in one keystroke.

Your files stay yours. Screenshots upload to your own Google Drive. MyShot runs no servers and never stores your captures.

How it works

  • Press a shortcut, select a region
  • MyShot saves it locally and uploads it to your Google Drive
  • A shareable link lands on your clipboard

Platforms

Linux (X11 and Wayland)Supported
WindowsSupported
macOSPlanned for a later release

Privacy in one line

MyShot requests the narrowest Google Drive permission that exists (drive.file), which lets it see only the files it created itself — never the rest of your Drive. It collects no analytics and sends nothing to us.

Status

MyShot is in active development and not yet generally available.
